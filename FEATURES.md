# Emerald Herald Features

This list reflects the design targets for the project. For full details, see `docs/GAMEDESIGN.md`.

## Core Pillars

- Challenging but fair battles with strong player tools
- High replayability via seeded Curses and encounters
- Competitive-grade NPC teams (items, EVs, movesets)
- Quality-of-life first to minimize grinding
- Preserve Emerald’s story beats while elevating gameplay

## Signature Systems

- **Bearer of the Curse**: 3 Banes + 3 Boons from a seed-based roll
- **Legacy Dungeons**: three optional, high-difficulty dungeons that remove a Bane
- **Seeded Encounters**: 20% of wild slots unique to each run
- **Item Restrictions**: no mid-battle healing item spam; fair rules for NPCs

## Battle & Difficulty

- Full competitive teams for major fights (EVs, items, optimized moves)
- Smarter AI and coverage-based decision making
- Level curve tuned for challenge without grinding

## Pokemon Availability

- Gen 1–7 roster with regional forms
- Early access to strong Pokemon and competitive movesets
- Story legendaries remain narrative-locked; others appear via dungeons/encounters

## Quality of Life

- Physical/Special split
- Infinite TMs
- Early Rare Candies for fast leveling
- HM field move removal
- Toggleable EXP Share
- Fast text and streamlined UI
- Early Move Relearner and Move Deleter

## Postgame (Planned)

- Elite Four rematch with level 95–100 teams
- Superboss house with themed Level 100 fights
- Optional stretch-goal dungeon (The Sealed Chamber)
